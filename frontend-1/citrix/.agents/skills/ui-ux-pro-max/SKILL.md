# UI/UX Pro Max — Design Intelligence Guidelines

## 1. Typography System
- **Display & Headings**: `Plus Jakarta Sans` or `Outfit` (wght 600, 700, 800) for authoritative, modern architectural feeling.
- **Interface & Body**: `Inter` (wght 400, 500, 600) with tight tracking (`tracking-tight` for titles, `tracking-normal` for body).
- **Data & Numeric**: `JetBrains Mono` for timeslots, room IDs, capacity counts, and solver telemetry.

## 2. Color Science & Contrast
- **Canvas / Background**: Deep Obsidian `#070B14` and Rich Navy `#0B1120`.
- **Card Surfaces**: Layered translucent slate `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.75)` with subtle `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` borders and soft ambient blur (16px).
- **Primary Brand**: Gradient between Luminous Electric Indigo (`#6366F1`) and Sky Sapphire (`#0EA5E9`).
- **Teal / Energy Accent**: Cyber Cyan (`#06B6D4`) and Mint (`#10B981`).
- **Status Palettes (Refined & Non-Garish)**:
  - Available: Emerald/Mint `#10B981` (`bg-emerald-500/15 text-emerald-300 border-emerald-500/30`)
  - Occupied: Coral Rose `#F43F5E` (`bg-rose-500/15 text-rose-300 border-rose-500/30`)
  - Partial: Sun Amber `#F59E0B` (`bg-amber-500/15 text-amber-300 border-amber-500/30`)
  - Pending: Amethyst `#A855F7` (`bg-purple-500/15 text-purple-300 border-purple-500/30`)

## 3. Visual Restraint
- Do not alter layout geometry, button placements, navigation, or functional workflows.
- Strictly enhance font weights, letter spacing, font families, and color luminosity.
